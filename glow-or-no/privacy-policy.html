01 — Introduction

Privacy Policy for Glow or No

Glow or No is an AI-powered photo setup feedback application operated by Centaur Studios.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the Glow or No app. We value your privacy and only process information as necessary to provide our service.

02 — Information We Collect

Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide the Glow or No features:

  • Photos or videos/images: Images selected by you for analysis within the app.
  • Analysis results: The feedback and scores generated by the app.
  • Local history: Your analysis history is stored on your device.
  • Technical data: Basic technical data may be processed by servers as necessary to provide the service.

We do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, payment information, or health data.

03 — How We Use Information

How We Use Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide AI photo analysis
  • To compare selected photos in the Best Photo Picker feature
  • To generate retake suggestions and platform fit feedback
  • To operate and improve app reliability
  • To respond to support requests if you contact us

04 — AI Processing

AI Processing

Glow or No relies on artificial intelligence to provide feedback.

Data Processing and Third-Party AI Services:

In order to provide our core photo analysis functionality, Glow or No securely transmits user-selected photos to a third-party AI service provider (OpenAI). By using the photo analysis feature, you explicitly consent to this data transmission.

Your photos are processed exclusively to generate automated, personalized feedback regarding your photo. We do not store your photos on our servers after the analysis is complete. They are immediately deleted, and your photos are NEVER used to train, improve, or fine-tune any AI models.

The app is designed to critique the photo setup, not the person. The AI is specifically instructed not to judge physical attractiveness, beauty, body, identity, age, race, gender, skin, or health.

05 — Data Sharing

Data Sharing

We respect your data privacy:

  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not share photos for advertising or tracking.
  • Photos may be processed by service providers only to provide app functionality.

06 — Data Retention

Data Retention

We minimize data retention to what is necessary:

  • Analysis history may be stored locally on your device.
  • You can clear your history directly within the app.
  • Server-side processing is intended only to generate the requested analysis.

07 — Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy

Glow or No is not directed to children under the age of 13. Furthermore, users should not upload images of others without their permission.

08 — User Choices

User Choices

You have control over how you use the app:

  • You can choose not to upload photos.
  • You can clear your analysis history in Settings.
  • You can revoke photo permissions in iOS Settings at any time.
  • You can contact support with any privacy concerns.

09 — Security

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data. However, please be aware that no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10 — Changes to This Policy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11 — Contact Us
